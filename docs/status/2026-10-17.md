Ready for security review. (PR #262)
- S3: a tenant's action backlog is capped: `POST .../sprouts/actions` is refused as a whole with `429 backlog_full` (details `pending`, `limit`, `requested`, `available`; `Retry-After: 60`; nothing written) when the tenant's queued and dispatching items plus the request's asset_ids exceed `SAASAPI_ACTION_MAX_PENDING_PER_TENANT` (default 2000, 1 to 100000; Helm `saasapi.actionDispatch.maxPendingPerTenant`; a soft limit; update rollouts never refused). Dispatch defaults raised in saasapi and farmer: cmd.run/cook pool 256, tenant cap 32, self_update 16; `SAASAPI_OUTBOX_ACTION_MAX_AGE` 30m. Saas migration 00009 adds `idx_asset_action_items_tenant_status`. Ready for security review. (PR #265)
- S4: a cook has a render budget of its own (security review B4): every render of one cook, include discovery and step building together, is charged to total render time (`IMAS_RECIPE_COOK_RENDER_TIMEOUT`, 10s, from `renderTimeout` to 5m), total rendered bytes (`IMAS_RECIPE_COOK_MAX_RENDERED_BYTES`, 8 MiB, up to 256 MiB) and total range iterations (`IMAS_RECIPE_COOK_MAX_RANGE_ITERATIONS`, 100000, up to 100000000); a cook over it fails with `ErrCookRenderBudget` before anything is staged. The include cap is a setting, `IMAS_RECIPE_MAX_INCLUDES` (default 64, 1 to 256); saasapi gets only that one and refuses an upload whose own include list is longer (`422 recipe_too_many_includes`). Helm keys under `farmer.recipes.templateLimits`. Ready for security review. (PR #266)
- S5: farmer and saasapi authenticate to Valkey as user `default` with a password the OpenBao bootstrap Job generates once (property `valkey_password` of the seed path, check-and-set, never overwritten), delivered by ESO as Secret `imas-valkey-auth` and mounted as a file only (`IMAS_VALKEY_PASSWORD_FILE`, `SAASAPI_VALKEY_PASSWORD_FILE`); the bundled Valkey and its replicas use the same Secret; `valkey.auth.enabled=false` renders only with `valkey.auth.allowInsecure=true`. The clients can use TLS (`*_VALKEY_TLS`, a CA from a ConfigMap, TLS 1.2 or later, never the system roots), the bundled Valkey runs `maxmemory-policy noeviction`, and both clients warn at startup when Valkey may evict (`internal/valkeyconn`). The UAT runs with the password (`saasapi-secrets.sh` delivers the Secret; `check.sh` checks NOAUTH without it). Ready for security review. (PR #267)
- S6: an offboarded tenant's data is erased after a retention delay (`SAASAPI_TENANT_PURGE_AFTER`, default 720h, Helm `saasapi.tenantPurge`) or at once through `POST /v1/operator/tenants/{tenant_id}/purge`: a sealed `internal.tenant.purge` job (new outbox job type `purge`, migration saas 00010) makes farmer delete the tenant's Valkey keys, farmer-schema rows, objects (recipes, recipe audit, staged recipes, job output, sprout JWTs) and KV keypair, and destroy its two Transit keys, then saasapi deletes its saas rows and sets it `purged`. Kept: a tombstone (saas `tenants` row, `provisioning_jobs`, farmer's deleted `pki_tenants` row, a minimal locked-out Account JWT). Guards, step order, resume, one audit entry with counts only, and an inventory test (`internal/tenantpurge`) that fails on any undecided table, object root, Valkey prefix or KV user. farmer's Transit policy may now destroy keys (`deletion_allowed="true"` only), its object store user may delete sprout JWTs, and its tenantbox policy may delete a tenant's KV keypair (metadata `delete` on `<kvPath>/tenants/+` only); `farmer.openbao.natsSign.retiredTenantKeyRetentionDays` removed. Ready for security review. (PR #268)
