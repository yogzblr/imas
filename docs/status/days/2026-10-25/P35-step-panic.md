change: P35: a panic in an ingredient step (a nil map, a bad type assertion, a COM wrapper bug) no longer crashes the sprout. The cook path recovers it around every step's Test and Apply, on_exit steps included, and fails that step with an error naming the step, ingredient, method and mode, the panic value and the panicking goroutine's stack (8 frames); the job completes and later steps run. A Windows step bounded by IMAS_WINDOWS_STEP_TIMEOUT runs in a goroutine of its own, so it recovers there too, and a step abandoned at the bound that panics later still releases its fence, so later steps of the same ingredient are not blocked (review on #320). Proven by unit tests with fake panicking cookers, run with -race.
